These rules describe approved behavior delivered behind flags; they do not
establish production availability.

## WW-01 — Week-end deadline

Status: agreed; available to every active member. Goals are due at the end of the selected
week. No per-goal date picker is required. Tasks are acceptable when the intended
result is clear. Members enter recurring work by hand for each week; there is no copy-from-last-week control. Updates and Goals tabs sit beside the date navigation. Updates contains today’s statuses and previous updates; Goals contains goal management and the same four daily status choices for the current week, synchronized with Updates. Switching tabs retains drafts while autosave continues. Each goal carries its latest saved status forward within its week; new goals remain unselected until a status is chosen. Daily messages start empty on a new day, including when the tab stays open overnight. Unsent or failed previous-day edits remain separately available with their original date and Retry; recovering them does not replace today’s message. Completed goals cannot be reordered and remain last under Completed in Goals. Today’s update shows goals completed today, while goals already Done before today stay hidden from its list. Saved daily snapshots retain carried statuses. Their original history is preserved. Changing Done to another status in Goals returns the goal to Today’s update.

## WW-02 — Goal visibility and public daily updates

Status: agreed, behind `weekly-goal-privacy`. A member chooses Team (default), Me
and my career lead, or Only me for each weekly goal. This covers text, details,
links and statuses. TV always gets only team goals. Historical snapshots satisfy
both original and latest saved visibility, including removed goals. Hidden controls
never widen saved access. Daily update bodies are always shared with the team and
TV. The daily composer does not show a sharing notice. Sharing a team goal shares its linked focus title and
target wording, without sharing the focus document.

## WW-03 — Stable targets and member-confirmed progress

Status: agreed, behind `weekly-focus-links`. Draft links target the whole focus.
Confirmed-focus links may target an objective or SMART goal by locked version and
position. The focus shows reverse links with the same visibility protection.
On track suggests In progress; At risk and Blocked suggest Needs attention; Done
suggests Achieved. Only the member's explicit Save changes focus progress. The
member can edit or cancel the suggestion. No automatic rollup or external completion.

## WW-04 — Personal development review remains deferred

Status: deferred (#206). Keep saved focus drafts, including personal development,
visible to the member and career lead, and send the whole focus for review. Add
clear guidance. Separate approval/privacy rules for personal development remain
an open owner decision.
