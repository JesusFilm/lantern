# Lantern and Quantum: overlap, differentiation, and adoption

Research date: 15 October 2026 (Pacific/Auckland).

Historical comparison: Lantern delivery/scope statements below describe the earlier guide-beta snapshot and were superseded later that day by the accepted authoring application and team-trial reconciliation. See [current PRD](../PRD.md) and [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md); this research does not reopen deferred features.

## Scope and confidence

This comparison provisionally assumes “Quantum” means Quantum Workplace. The user has not confirmed the vendor or supplied a tenant URL. Public sources cannot establish the organization's purchased modules, configuration, usage, or reasons for non-use. “Minimalist organization” is also ambiguous: a minimal rollout, a flat organization, and a ministry organization are different propositions. None alone establishes product uniqueness.

## Assessment

Lantern addresses a subset of the same problem space: clarifying contribution, agreeing goals, and supporting development conversations. The defensible hypothesis is a simpler, locally appropriate position-focus experience, not an entirely new product category. Whether that experience justifies another maintained system remains unproven. An unused existing tool is evidence of an adoption gap, not evidence of a missing capability.

Quantum supports configurable conversation agendas, templates, and participants, including collaborators where organization settings permit. Its Goal Review booster connects goals to conversations. Therefore, a tailored position-focus process could potentially fit Quantum; this is an inference to validate in the actual tenant, not a verified equivalence. Sources: [Create 1-on-1s](https://help.quantumworkplace.com/how-to-start-a-new-1-on-1), [Goal Review booster](https://help.quantumworkplace.com/booster-goal-review).

## What could explain non-use?

Follow-up evidence from the user on 15 October 2026: account setup and unclear ownership are the clearest observed obstacles; users have not explicitly rejected Quantum. Prioritize these findings over the speculative explanations below. The [goal-guidance review](quantum-goal-guidance-review.md) examines teaching support separately.

These are competing hypotheses, not findings:

| Hypothesis | Evidence that would discriminate |
|---|---|
| Feature overload or navigation friction | Observe users find their focus, edit a goal and return to it; record wrong turns, assistance and time. |
| Wrong modules or configuration | Ask the internal owner which modules are licensed/enabled, which templates exist and which permissions apply. |
| Process mismatch | Try a real career-lead relationship, qualitative development outcome, joint agreement and revision in a test template. |
| No accountable owner or review habit | Identify who initiates conversations and follows up, and whether that practice exists outside the software. |
| Low trust or unclear visibility | Ask users who they believe can read records and what decisions those records affect; compare with actual settings. |
| Duplicate recording | Identify other required records and whether entering goals in Quantum adds a second administrative task. |

Quantum's vendor-published [Anders case study](https://www.quantumworkplace.com/success-story-anders) describes adopting the coaching philosophy before the software supported it. This illustrates a possible process-before-tool explanation, but is promotional evidence and does not diagnose this organization.

## Recommended bounded evaluation

Before expanding Lantern's workflow implementation, compare the smallest reasonable Quantum configuration against Lantern's prototype and a plain document. Use the same fictional or consented example and equivalent guidance, avoiding an unfair comparison between a tailored prototype and an untailored suite.

Suggested tasks: find the current position focus; understand mission/objectives; edit one goal; discuss it with the career lead; record agreement; revisit an earlier baseline; identify who can read it. Record setup effort, participant effort, help needed, clarity of the resulting document, visibility understanding and willingness to return. These are evaluation proposals, not added project requirements or an authorization to change the organization's tenant.

Possible decisions:

- Configure Quantum if the essential experience fits with tolerable setup and use.
- Retain Lantern as guidance/prototyping material if teaching is the main gap, while avoiding duplicate personnel records.
- Continue a narrow standalone Lantern if there is a repeatable, material interaction or process mismatch and an owner accepts ongoing hosting, access, recovery and maintenance work.
- Improve the coaching process first if the main failure is ownership, trust or follow-through; another tool will not resolve those by itself.

The strongest prospective Lantern positioning is: a focused workspace for ministry teams to agree, read and revisit a person's contribution and development. That is a useful hypothesis, not a market-wide uniqueness claim.
