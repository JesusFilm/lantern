# January position-focus roadmap

The career lead accepted these five milestones as a starting roadmap on 13 October 2026. They are recorded outcomes awaiting detailed planning, not implementation tickets. The working deadline is 31 January 2027; the exact date and whether completion requires submission, agreement or manager approval remain open.

| Milestone | Target window | Relative effort | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agree the position-focus guide | October 2026 | Small | [Milestone](../.scratch/position-focus-guide/milestone.md) |
| Know each engineer’s current situation | October–November 2026 | Medium | [Milestone](../.scratch/engineer-context/milestone.md) |
| Engineers can complete a position focus | November–early December 2026 | Largest | [Milestone](../.scratch/position-focus-submission/milestone.md) |
| Review and clarify together | December 2026–January 2027 | Medium | [Milestone](../.scratch/position-focus-review/milestone.md) |
| Complete the first team cycle | By 31 January 2027 (provisional) | Small–medium | [Milestone](../.scratch/first-team-cycle/milestone.md) |

Aim to make the submission workflow usable by early December, leaving January for review and clarification. Catch-ups and preparation of the guide can proceed alongside software development. Relative effort is not a delivery estimate.

Each milestone folder holds its outcome in milestone.md and planning map in map.md. Planning resolves open decisions; to-spec records an agreed specification; to-tickets produces an approved implementation breakdown. The milestones are ordered for delivery planning, but their order does not assert that every activity is blocked by the preceding milestone. Detailed dependency edges are set during ticket planning.

After January, consider development goals, ongoing reviews and selected read-only summaries for the engineering manager and delivery lead. Automated messaging, feedback collection and transcription follow demonstrated needs.
