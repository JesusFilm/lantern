# Refine 2 — Guide and account

Derived from Refine 1. Position Focus remains unchanged. Guide uses short objective lessons (purpose, definition, focus) and one SMART quality per step, with next/previous and direct step navigation. Each lesson pairs a weak and stronger example with a brief explanation and a schematic visual. Examples isolate the quality being taught; the complete example combines them in one generic objective and two goals. Dates and targets are illustrative.

Account applies the same read-first spacing and typography with an edit-name dialog. Cancel discards edits; Save retains them in temporary memory. Hosted browser verified lesson sequence, full example, and profile save/cancel. No new account infrastructure.
