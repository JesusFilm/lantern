# Lantern participation

Lantern distinguishes permission to sign in from membership and career-lead authority.

## Language

**Approved participant**:
A person whose exact verified Google email has been approved for a particular Lantern environment. Approval permits eligible sign-in; it does not grant membership or career-lead authority.

**Active member**:
A participant with current membership in the team who can work on their own position focuses. Ending membership removes team access while preserving their records.

**Career lead**:
The team's designated member who can read current members' focuses and confirm an agreed baseline. Being an approved participant alone does not confer this role.

**QA participant**:
An approved participant using stage to verify the trial with synthetic focus content. A person may use distinct lead and member accounts to exercise those roles independently.
