## Outcome and revised scope

The local application provides persisted position-focus authoring and the accepted Refine 4 guide experience. Jaco said he was very happy with this minimal initial implementation. This closes the baseline against that accepted scope, not every requirement in the original guide beta.

Delivered: dated focus list and compact read view; metadata, objective and SMART-goal dialogs; Normal, Personal Development and Leadership objective types; account-name editing; a three-step objective guide, five-step SMART guide and concise generic example with one objective and two goals; PostgreSQL persistence, ownership isolation and optimistic conflict handling. The local walkthrough uses a shared local account and is not a multi-user trial.

The original broad mission-context/gap/review/timing teaching and full engineering example were intentionally superseded. No missing feature is implied by preserving their historical decision records.
