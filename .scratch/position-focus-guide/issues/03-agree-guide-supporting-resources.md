### Experience and authoring

- A visible help/guidance indicator in the position-focus experience opens optional, user-initiated walkthroughs. Critical mission objectives and SMART goals are independently selectable areas, each presenting small steps with Next transitions.
- Use professional, minimalist visuals, illustrations and examples where helpful. The application controls typography, layout, navigation, transitions and animation. Department resource editors supply heading and body text; visual customization and a slide/template builder are outside the initial delivery.
- Include department editing and a department selector from the start, with engineering as the initial populated full example. Other departments can add their own content later.
- Each department starts with shared default walkthrough text. Its career lead can adapt wording and examples to the department. Automatic propagation of later default-text changes into adapted resources has not been agreed.

### Mission context and teaching

- Use **organizational mission → department contribution → individual objectives**, with one organizational mission, rather than separate missions at each level. Retain the supplied organizational mission wording from the content decision.
- A reader-controlled collapsible context banner remains accessible across walkthrough steps, showing the organizational mission and department contribution where supplied and omitting missing context. A missing context statement does not create a requirement to duplicate or invent it. The required individual critical mission objective remains unchanged.
- The mission-objectives walkthrough teaches identifying whom someone serves, the intended outcome and the contribution’s connection to the mission, then expressing an objective. Include internal contributions and discovery when direction is unclear.
- The SMART walkthrough improves a vague goal through a worked example explaining all five qualities. Use observable evidence without forcing numerical targets where they do not fit. Consider all five qualities without requiring five written sections; some may be clear from context or established during career-lead review.
- The same SMART approach applies to mission objectives and to personal-development objectives and leadership development assignments when included. The resources must support understanding of drafting, joint review and lock-in as already agreed in the review-and-completion decision.

### Full example and optional development content

- There is no mandatory organizational format. Choose clear headings covering the agreed content, using the [working example already shared with engineering](../references/position-focus-working-example.png) as a reference.
- Develop its product-work scenario into the full engineering example, including gaps and next steps. Use shorter walkthrough examples for internal contributions and discovery. The reference’s counts, month markers and scenario details are illustrative, not universal requirements.
- Personal development remains optional and encouraged. Add a separate optional **Leadership development assignments** section beneath it, pairing a coach-given assignment with its specific goals and outcomes. This is an agreed addition to the earlier content decision.
- Both optional sections stay invisible until added through a discreet **Add optional section** control. Both the drafter and career lead can add and edit them; their contents are agreed together during review before lock-in. No additional approver is introduced.
- Explain the optional development sections in the resources so readers understand when to use them; keep the optional nature clear in examples.

### Acceptance and verification

- Someone unfamiliar with the process can prepare an objective with SMART goals, record gaps and next steps, and explain review and lock-in using the resources.
- They can find relevant examples and understand when the optional development sections are useful.
- A career lead can adapt departmental guidance and examples through text editing without design or animation skills.
- Verify these criteria through a practical walkthrough once the resources exist. Verification has not yet occurred; no numerical success target or fixed participant count is prescribed.
